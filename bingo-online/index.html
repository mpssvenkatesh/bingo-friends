Connecting…

1. One person creates a room and shares the 4-letter code.

2. Everyone arranges 1–25 on their own grid and presses Ready. Up to 15 players per room.

3. Take turns: on your turn, tap a number to call it. It gets struck on everyone's grid.

4. Each full row, column or diagonal strikes a letter. Strike them all and press the call button first to win.

Modes: 5 × 5 uses 1–25 and BINGO (5 lines). 7 × 7 uses 1–49 and FRIENDS (7 lines). 10 × 10 uses 1–100 and BINGO CHIPS (10 lines). The host picks the mode in the room.
